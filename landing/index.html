TeleNotch

A minimal teleprompter for Mac that sits right below your camera. Read your script while looking directly at the lens.

Download for Mac

macOS 14.0+ · Apple Silicon · Free

Features

Always on top

Floats above Zoom, Meet, or any app. Your script is always visible.

Ghost mode

Invisible to screen sharing so your audience only sees you, not the script.

Keyboard control

Space to play/pause, arrows for speed. No mouse needed during recording.

Completely local

No accounts, no internet required. Your scripts never leave your Mac.
